Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-nostalgic-a-childhood-625d3363): one sentence, at most 280 characters, that brings back a childhood memory.

The program writes a single present-tense vignette — one scene at dusk, one small object (cassava), one figure (mother), one animal (the cat) — to `/output/entry.txt`.

> At dusk your mother peels cassava on the back step while the neighbour's radio plays a song she hums without knowing the words, and the cat curls into the warm spot where your father's sandals were.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
